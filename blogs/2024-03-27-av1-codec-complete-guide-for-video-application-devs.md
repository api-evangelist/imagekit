AOMedia Video 1 (AV1) is a royalty-free, open-source video codec developed to be a successor to VP9 by the Alliance for Open Media (AOMedia). It is a state-of-the-art codec that addresses the rising demand for high-quality and high-resolution video delivery across web and mobile platforms.
